abstract: >-
  We build a multi-stage NLP pipeline over 14.7 million Swiss job ads, combining LLM
  extraction with hand-validated evaluation and semantic similarity matching (BGE/FAISS)
  to O*NET tasks, to produce firm-occupation-year measures of AI exposure. Linking these
  to the Swiss Household Panel via ISCO codes and anonymized firm IDs, we estimate
  effects on voting, economic outcomes, and political beliefs.
