abstract: >-
  Net automation and augmentation exposure across U.S. commuting zones (1952–2024)
  strongly predicts anti-incumbent voting in the post-war era, but the relationship
  collapses to zero in the modern period despite continued worker displacement. I
  develop a political translation framework and use LLM-based classification of party
  platforms to test observability and attribution channels.
